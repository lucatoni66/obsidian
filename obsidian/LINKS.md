### 1. Use the Hacker News Algolia Search (Best for exact date filtering)

The official HN search engine powered by Algolia allows you to filter stories by exact timeframes.

- Go to **[hn.algolia.com](https://hn.algolia.com/)**
    
- Click on **Search** and set the type to **Stories**.
    
- Under the **By Date** filter dropdown, select **Past 24 Hours** (or custom range for today).
    
- Alternatively, you can sort them by **Popularity** or **Date** to see what's trending or just dropping today.
